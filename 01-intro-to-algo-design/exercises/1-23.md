# Exercise 1.23

> *[3]* How many hours are one million seconds? How many days? Answer these questions by doing all arithmetic in your head.

Roughly 275 hours (in my head).

Reasoning: 
- 60*60 = 3600 seconds in an hour. 
- 10000/3600 ~= 2.75
- 1M / 10k = 100
- 2.75 * 100 = 275

That would also make 11.5 days

- 275/24 
- = 240/24 + 24/24 + 10/24
- = 10 + 1 + 10/24
- ~= 11.5

Actual answer is 277.777... hours

And 11.57 days
