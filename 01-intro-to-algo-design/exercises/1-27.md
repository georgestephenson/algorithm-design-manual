# Exercise 1.27

> *[3]* How long would it take to empty a bathtub with a drinking straw?

A full bath tub would take approx $0.5m * 1.5m * 0.5m = 0.375m^3$ of water.

I reckon I could drink 500ml of water through a straw in 4 to 8 seconds. Let's say 6 seconds.

But to *empty* a full bathtub - I'd have to spit the water out again! Rather than drink it. That might take a full 20 seconds.

So for one litre that's 40 seconds. There are 375 liters in the bathtub, which would give 15000 seconds, 250 minutes or 4 hours 10 minutes.

If you could use the straw as a siphon, it might be much faster, perhaps four times faster, roughly one hour. You'd need other tools to help siphon using a straw when you get to the bottom of the tub.
