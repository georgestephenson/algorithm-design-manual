# Exercise 1.26

> [3] How many Starbucks or McDonald's locations are there in your country?

## Geographic estimate

Every town of 100,000 people at this point likely has 2 McDonald's and potentially 2 Starbucks also. 

I'll put McDonald's at 2.1 per 100k and Starbucks at 1.7.

With a population of approx. 59 million,

Number of McDonald's = (59M / 100k) * 2.1 = 1239 McDonald's restaurants

Number of Starbucks = (59M / 100k) * 1.7 = 1003 Starbucks coffee shops

## Demographic estimate

### McDonald's

Of a population of 59 million, the McDonald's eating population may be 45 million - kids love it as well as adults.

Not everyone eats it that often, the average might be once per 3 weeks of those that eat it.

Each restaurant might serve 1000 people each day.

To serve 15 million each week, or 2.14 million each day, that would require 2140 restaurants.

### Starbucks

There is a smaller population that would drink Starbucks, perhaps 25 million. Some people will drink it every day which will drink the average visits up. The average could be as high as one Starbucks visit per 2 weeks for those 25 million.

Starbucks don't serve as many orders at once. Maybe 800 per day.

To serve 12.5 million per week, or 1.79 million per day, would require 2238 restaurants.

## Actual answer

My estimated range of UK McDonald's restaurants was 1239-2140

Actual answer is more than 1270 according to [McDonald's](https://www.mcdonalds.com/gb/en-gb/newsroom/history.html)

McDonald's say they serve 3.8 million customers each days, almost double my estimate. They must serve more like 2750 customers per day per restaurant.

My estimate range of Starbucks coffee shops was 1003-2238

Actual answer is 1214 according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starbucks#Locations)
