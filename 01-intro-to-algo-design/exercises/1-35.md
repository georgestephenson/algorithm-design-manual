# Exercise 1.35

> *[3]* How many gas stations are there in the United States?

I will guess this from memory in the spirit of the exercise...

## Population-based estimate

There are around 350 million people in the US. I'll guess 225 million are economically active adults. Some people will live in cities and not drive, but the US tends to be very car-centric. I'll subtract 25 million for public transport users. Another 25 million might be dependent on others.

Now do 175 million all drive their own car, or share a car? I'll subtract another 50 million for sharing cars, for 125 million actively used cars. But I'll need to add another 75 millions for work vehicles, buses and taxis.

Of those 200 million vehicles, on average they might drive 40 miles a day. If a tank lasts 200 miles, they need to fill up once per (working) week, roughly. It might take 10 minutes for each vehicle to fill up.

200 million * 10 minutes = 2 billion minutes of pumping per week.

An average gas station might have 8 pumps, and might be open 12 hours a day on average. They are never bumper-to-bumper busy, we might say 75% of the pumps are unoccupied on average.

2 pumps * 12 * 60 * 7 = 10080 minutes of active pumping per station per week

2000000000 / 10080 = 198,413 gas stations in the US.

## Geographic estimate

Gas stations are placed strategically across different roads and towns. Unfortunately I don't know how many roads and towns are in the US.

I could guess there are 50 states, maybe 20 cities and 50 larger towns in each state.

Each city on average could have 50 gas stations, each time might have 20.

That would give $50*20*50*2$ = 100,000 gas stations.

## Actual answer

According to [Fuel Express](https://www.fuelexpress.net/blog/general-information/how-many-gas-stations-are-in-the-us/) there are approximately 160,000 gas stations. My estimates of 100,000-198,413 were the right range!

US population is currently 342 million. Economically active labour force is 170 million.

There are 19,500 "incorporated places" in the US but cities vary widely in size.
