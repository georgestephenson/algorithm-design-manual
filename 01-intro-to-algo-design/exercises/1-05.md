## Part A

For $S = \{1,2,5,9,10\}, T = 5$,

The first-fit algorithm doesn't find a solution. It picks $1$ and $2$ which equals to $3$ and then none of the later numbers in the set fit.

The correct solution is $\{5\}$.

## Part B

For $S = \{1,2,5,9,10\}, T = 5$,

The best-fit algorithm fares the same as first-fit. In this case smallest to largest is the same order as left to right order. It doesn't have to be, but in terms of counterexamples the same counterexample works.

## Part C

If put the elements of $S$ in the knapsack from largest to smallest, this algorithm would succeed in the previous example.

For $S = \{1,2,5,9,10\}, T = 14$

Then largest to smallest fails to find a solution.

- Puts 10 in the knapsack first
- Then 2 is the next one that fits
- Then 1

This algorithm will put a sum of 13 in the knapsack and will fail to add up to exactly $T = 14$

The correct solution is $\{5,9\}$.
