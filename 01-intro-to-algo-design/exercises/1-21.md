# Exercise 1.21

> *[3]* Do all the books you own total at least one million pages? How many total pages are stored in your school library?

I own roughly 150 books, on average I would estimate 350 pages each. $150 \times 350 = 52,500$, so short of one million by a long stretch.

From memory when I went to school 14 years ago, my school had about six big long bookshelves about seven feet tall and six feet wide, and about ten shorter bookshelves, maybe four feet tall and six feet wide.

If each book on average is about an inch, which on average is about 400 pages, and each foot of bookshelf height is one shelf, that would be:

$(6 \times 7 + 10 \times 4) \times 6 \times 12 \times 400 = 2,361,600$

Well my one bookshelf certainly isn't as big as half of the whole school library!
